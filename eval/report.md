> **All 48 evaluation files** (`12 Digital PDFs`, `12 Digital Visual PDFs`, `12 Scanned PDFs`, `12 JPG Images`) plus `images/egypt_00001.png` are **100% synthetic** (`test_data = True`, generated by `make_samples.py` / `make_pdfs.py`). **Real scanned commercial registry samples: `0` (< 20).**

## 1. Split Policy (No Blind Tuning)
- **Dev Split (`samples 01–08`):** 8 underlying records × 4 formats (32 files). Used for failure analysis, threshold selection, and CV ablation.
- **Held-Out Split (`samples 09–12`):** 4 underlying records × 4 formats (16 files). Never inspected or tuned on; used strictly to verify generalization.

> **Honest Revert Note:** Disabling `clahe` and `denoise` simultaneously looked promising on the 2-image dev ablation, so we tested it on the held-out split (`eval/v03_final_heldout.json`). On held-out, disabling both **regressed** digit accuracy from **98.65% → 92.79%**, dropped Clean Image `capital` from `100% → 0%`, and caused `sample_10_scan.jpg` to lose `issue_date` (`100% → 0%`). Both `clahe=True` and `denoise=True` were therefore **reverted to ON**; only `conditional_sharpen=False` and `generate_variant_b=False` were kept disabled.

## 6. Digit Confusion Summary (Quality-Passing Docs)
- **Baseline (48 docs):** `1353/1780 = 76.01%` — top errors were missing digits (`'0' -> <MISSING>`: 101, `'2' -> <MISSING>`: 66, `'0' -> <DEL>`: 29) caused by CRAFT/PaddleOCR dropping Arabic-Indic zeros `٠` and missing entire date/capital fields.
- **v0.3 Held-Out (quality-passing docs):** `219/222 = 98.65%` — only 3 residual digit errors across all 6 quality-passing held-out documents (`<INS>` / `<DEL>` of single digits on `sample_11_phone` capital and `sample_10_scan` tax ID), **all flagged with `needs_review=True`**.
